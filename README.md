## Overview

**Profiler X³ (A.C.E.)** is an analytical workbench engineered for Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) analysts, digital forensics investigators, and Trust & Safety researchers. Unlike traditional stylometric tools that rely solely on surface-level word frequencies, A.C.E. triangulates **36+ cross-domain forensic sensors** across psycholinguistics, L1 syntactic interference, circadian/ritual chronography, network latency physics, and structural semiotics.

Built around a strict **Epistemic Humility (Honest Abstention)** architecture: every sensor explicitly defines its evidentiary caveats, confidence bounds, and triggers an automatic refusal when sample size or register is insufficient—eliminating false-positive attribution.

## Core Architectural Pillars

### 1. Unified `Stream Core` & Normalized Observation Layer
* Processes raw text, multi-party chat logs, and timestamped message streams into a single forensic substrate.
* Extracts baseline telemetry (`FK per sentence`, `typos per timestamp`, `addressee topology`, `production-chain markers`) and routes standardized observations into the **Evidence Registry**.
* **Contradiction & Conflict Engine (`Heuer ACH`):** Automatically flags cross-domain anomalies (e.g., native Persian syntactic interference paired with European circadian sleep rhythms or LLM-sanitized surface prose).

### 2. Multi-Domain Forensic Matrix (36+ Analytical Sensors)
* **L1 Interference & Sociolinguistics:** `Shibboleth Trap` (uncontrolled motor/orthographic noise), `Lexical Parasite` (L1 discourse-marker calquing in FA/RU/EN), `Toponymic Compass` (critical geopolitics & atlas bias), `Migration Markers` (dialectal isoglosses).
* **Circadian & Geo-Temporal Forensics:** `Sundial Engine` (solar terminator alignment & pre-Iftar/fasting cognitive degradation windows), `Ariadne Latency` (network RTT physics vs. cognitive deception pauses), `Kairos Fracture` (chronopolitical time perception).
* **Deception, Stress & Masking Detection:** `Sanitizer Scars` (BPE/LLM translation seams & idiom erasure), `Alibi Friction` (Undeutsch/Vrij evasion index under accusation), `Palimpsest Memory` (pre/post-edit drift & XML revision archaeology), `Stress Differential` (cold vs. hot phase cultural regression).
* **Cognitive & Neuro-Rhythmic Profiling:** `Saccade Leap` (Luria/Andreasen semantic jump topology), `Prosodic Breath` (Sievers silent-reading syllable cadence), `Sensorium Prism` (visual/auditory/kinesthetic/olfactory metaphor mapping), `Acoustic Cadence` (quantitative rhythmic symmetry).
* **Group Dynamics & Threat Assessment:** `Hierarchy Tessellation` (T/V & *taarof* power graphs), `Asymmetric Entropy` (conversational energy balance), `Ouroboros Mirror` (sockpuppet & style-accommodation detection), `Gavin's Trigger` (MOSAIC-inspired irreversibility & actionability index), `Schismogenesis` (Bateson dehumanization escalation).

### 3. Counter-Attribution (`OPSEC Sandbox`)
* Interactive red-teaming module that highlights high-exposure idiosyncratic phrases, L1 calques, and punctuation fingerprints inside a draft text, allowing operators to sanitize and re-scan prior to publication.

### 4. Intelligence Standards & Interoperability
* Zero-dependency standalone browser architecture (operates completely offline in air-gapped environments).
* Native export to **STIX 2.1** (Structured Threat Information Expression), **JSON**, **Markdown**, and **Forensic PDF** with built-in PII redaction mode.

## Methodological Disclaimer & Limitations

Profiler X³ is an **investigative hypothesis-generation engine**, not an automated verdict machine. Every module displays explicit academic citations and methodological boundaries (*caveats*). Output indicators represent probabilistic behavioral and linguistic leads intended for corroboration with technical telemetry (DFIR/SIGINT/FININT).
